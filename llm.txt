## One2Talk Star & Success Award 2025

> One2Talk Star & Success Award 2025 is the biggest online voting award show for Malayalam cinema. Fans can vote for their favourite stars across 17 categories including Best Film, Best Actor, Best Actress, Best Director, Best Music Director, and more.

## About
One2Talk is an entertainment platform focused on Malayalam cinema. The Star & Success Award 2025 is a free online voting event where audiences can support their favourite films, actors, and technicians.
